# CHR-STD-04/06 — Entry into scientific research

This is the proposed bridge to CHR-MATH-00, not the scientific results or the full research repository charter.

Working title: Mathematics of Time in Synthetic Universes — Foundations of Computational Temporality, Causality and Experience.

Keep three independent authorities: historical evidence (archive), new research (private research repository proposed), normative temporal contracts (chronograf-spec). Research must not be constrained by current JSON/RCG representation choices; establish mathematical objects before choosing their encodings. Existing suffix-typed field names are used when writing protocol bindings, not in the mathematics itself.

## Research register

Use typed stable IDs: CHR-DEF, AX, LEM, THM, CONJ, HYP, CE, EXP, REF; requirement and conformance IDs are independent. Each record identifies statement, scope, assumptions, source and exact version, dependencies, status, proof/experiment evidence, review and supersession. Distinguish established literature, new definition, assumption, formally proved result, conjecture, empirically tested hypothesis, rejected hypothesis and unverified claim. Literature attribution and novelty review precede novelty claims.

Lean 4/mathlib is the proposed proof environment. Pin exact versions, verify reproducible builds, inspect theorem dependencies and axioms, prohibit sorry/admit in any claim of completed proof, distinguish legitimate declared mathematical assumptions from unreviewed custom axioms. A proof establishes consequences of its assumptions, not empirical truth of clocks, physics, cognition or experience. No Lean installation or proof execution is claimed by this RFC. Use counterexamples, independent reviews, simulation and preregistered experiments when appropriate. Preserve negative results.

## Sequence

CHR-MATH-00 establishes research charter, bibliography protocol, proposition register, Lean workspace, and compendium skeleton. CHR-MATH-01 surveys mathematical and metrological foundations. CHR-MATH-02 develops candidate models of event, domain, order, coordinate, duration and uncertainty; formalization can proceed in parallel where sound. Subsequent research on mappings, partial order, provenance, branching and THP should inform later Chronograf phases without precommitting results.

CHR-0.3B consumes accepted definitions and exact encoding decisions via a traceable contract proposal, not automatic promotion. Research can begin while architecture/grammar candidates are under review; no breaking schema changes or public normative release without acceptance. The proposed private chronograf-research repository needs creation and access verification before remote research work. No repository creation or release is performed here.
